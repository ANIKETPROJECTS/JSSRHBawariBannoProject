---
name: Verified customer reviews
description: Product expectations for customer-submitted reviews and public storefront visibility.
---

Customers can review only products included in their successfully paid orders. Allow one active review per customer and product; rejected reviews may be edited and resubmitted. Keep submitted reviews pending Admin approval, then show approved reviews and ratings to all storefront visitors.

**Why:** The user expects customers to review products they ordered, for reviews to be saved reliably, and for approved reviews to appear for everyone on the storefront.

**How to apply:** Verify purchase ownership on the server, store the customer association and verified-purchase status, expose only approved reviews publicly, and keep the Admin approval path connected to storefront review lists and summaries.
